# Support

## Getting help

- **Bug?** Open a [bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Feature idea?** Open a [feature request](../../issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Usage question?** Open a [question](../../issues/new?template=question.yml) or start a
  [Discussion](../../discussions) if enabled on this repository.
- **Security issue?** See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — do not open a public issue.

## Before asking

- Check the [README](README.md) for setup and configuration.
- Search existing [issues](../../issues) — your question may already be answered.
- Include your Janitor version, `DB_DRIVER`, Node.js version, and relevant logs
  (with secrets redacted) when asking for help.

## Response times

Janitor is maintained on a best-effort basis by volunteers. There's no SLA —
please be patient, and consider contributing a fix yourself (see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)) if you're able to.
